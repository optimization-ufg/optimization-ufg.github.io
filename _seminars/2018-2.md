    title: An complexity analysis of gradient descent for multiobjective optimization
    abstract: |
      An complexity analysis of gradient descent for multiobjective optimization is analyzed in this talk. In addition, the results of the paper &quot;Fliege, J.; A. I. F. Vaz; L. N. Vicente. Complexity of gradient descent for multiobjective optimization. (2018) &quot; will be discussed.
  - date: 2018-08-30
    time: 8:00
    speaker: Vando Antônio Adona
    affiliation: PhD student, IME/UFG
    title: A partially inexact generalized alternating direction method of multipliers and its iteration-complexity analysis
    abstract: |
      In this talk we will present the complexity analysis of a version partially inexact of the alternating direction method of multipliers for computing approximate solution of a linearly constrained convex optimization problem. Some numerical tests for the proposed method will also be illustrated.
  - date: 2018-09-06
    time: 8:00
    speaker: Leandro da Fonseca Prudente
    affiliation: IME/UFG
    title: A Wolfe line search algorithm for vector optimization
    abstract: |
      In a recent paper, Lucambio Pérez and Prudente extended the Wolfe conditions for the vector-valued optimization. In this talk, we will present a line search algorithm for finding a step-size satisfying the strong Wolfe conditions in the vector optimization setting. Well definiteness and finite termination results will be presented. We will discuss practical aspects related to the algorithm and present some numerical experiments illustrating its applicability.
  - date: 2018-09-13
    time: 8:00
    speaker: Luis Román Lucambio Pérez
    affiliation: IME/UFG
    title: Non-linear conjugate gradients algorithm in Riemannian Manifolds
    abstract: |
      We will present an example of Vector Optimization Problem on Riemannian Manifold, which, besides to be potentially useful, fulfils all hypothesis for global convergence of the by us proposed conjugate gradient methods with the vector extensions of the Fletcher—Reeves, Conjugate Descent, Dai—Yuan, Polak—Ribiére—Polyak and Hestenes—Stiefel parameters.
  - date: 2018-09-20
    time: 8:00
    speaker: Orizon Pereira Ferreira
    affiliation: IME/UFG
    joint: Sándor Z. Németh and Lianghai Xiao
    title: On the spherical quasi-convexity of quadratic functions
    abstract: |
      This talk is about the spherical quasi-convexity of quadratic functions on spherically convex sets. Several conditions characterizing the spherical quasi-convexity of quadratic functions are presented. In particular, conditions implying spherical quasi-convexity of quadratic functions on the spherical positive orthant are given. Some examples are provided as an application of the obtained results.
  - date: 2018-09-27
    time: 8:00
    speaker: Lucas Vidal de Meireles
    affiliation: PhD student, IME/UFG
    title: Inexact proximal point method for locally Lipschitz functions in multiobjective optimization
    abstract: |
      In this talk is presented a notion of approximate Pareto solution, which was introduced by Loridan in 1984, a condition necessary for characterizing such solution is studying for multiobjective programming. Moreover, exploiting these solutions formulate an inexact proximal point method for multiobjective optimization. The main convergence result is applied to locally Lipschitz functions. Also is proved full convergence to a weak Pareto optimal when the multiobjective program is convex.
