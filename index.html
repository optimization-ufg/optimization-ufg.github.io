The Continuous Optimization Group at the Federal University of Goiás (UFG) is affiliated with the Graduate Program in Mathematics at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics (IME).

Our current interests encompass both theoretical and computational aspects of nonlinear programming, including variational inequalities, multiobjective and vector optimization, generalized equations, and optimization on Riemannian manifolds. The group also works on combinatorial optimization and operations research, with applications to cutting and packing, vehicle routing, and scheduling problems.
