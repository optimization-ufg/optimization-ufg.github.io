Research

What we work on

The group is affiliated with the Graduate Program in Mathematics at the Institute of Mathematics and Statistics (IME) of the Federal University of Goiás (UFG), where its members supervise master's and doctoral students.

Nonlinear programming

Theory and algorithms for constrained and unconstrained problems, with convergence and complexity analysis.

Variational inequalities

Variational inequalities, generalized equations and monotone operator methods.

Multiobjective and vector optimization

Pareto optimality and descent methods for problems with several conflicting objectives.

Riemannian optimization

Optimization on Riemannian manifolds, including Hadamard manifolds and the geometry of algorithms.

Combinatorial optimization

Integer programming models and heuristics for hard discrete problems.

Operations research

Applications to cutting and packing, vehicle routing and scheduling.
